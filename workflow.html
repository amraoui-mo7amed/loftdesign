01 · The Network

The Five Roles & the Money Flow

Each level buys, resells, and books its own margin. Follow the coin as it travels from the factory floor to the end customer.

Provider

Makes the product, sets wholesale & retail.

LOFT

Curates, validates, books commission.

Affiliate

Resells via own branded store.

Semi

Sells into assigned client roster.

Client

Receives & pays retail.

The money rule

Every level buys at one price and resells at the next — its margin becomes its profit. When an order reaches Delivered, every level’s wallet is credited instantly in one automatic pass.

03 · The Product Lifecycle

From Factory to Client

Follow Oak Lounge Chair — three numbers drive the entire chain.

Pricing snapshot

20,000 DZD

Purchase · loft_purchase_price

Supplier's price / what Loft owes them per unit.

26,000 DZD

Wholesale · loft_wholesale_price

Default wholesale for affiliates buying from Loft.

35,000 DZD

Retail · loft_retail_price

Public price customers pay in the stores.

Provider
Create the product

Provider fills the form (thumbnail, category, description, gallery, variants) and the three price fields. As a supplier, no admin approval — it’s Approved & Active immediately on save.

System
Pricing rows are written

Two records are created/updated: SupplierPrice (purchase 20,000 — what Loft owes the provider) and LoftPrice (wholesale 26,000 / retail 35,000 — the defaults applied store-side).

Provider
Brand its store

Opens My Store, sets name, logo, banner, description & header color. The storefront goes public at /p/<username>/ and lists only the provider’s own active products.

Affiliate
Add it to the catalog

An affiliate created by this provider opens Available Products and — because they belong to the provider — only that provider’s products are visible. The affiliate prices its own catalog: purchase = provider wholesale 26,000, its retail = 35,000.

Affiliate
Resell via its store

The affiliate’s storefront /a/CODE/ now shows the chair. Anyone buying from that URL is tagged with the affiliate’s code on the order.

Provider
Update anytime

The provider edits the product & prices freely; the system syncs SupplierPrice/LoftPrice and logs every price change — nothing drifts out of sync.

Key rules

Provider products are instantly approved and fully self-managed (no admin gate). Admin-created products can be featured in the admin store. Visibility requires: product active + stock > 0 + the appropriate store flag.

04 · The Order Workflow

From Checkout to Cash

The chair is sold. Here is exactly how one order is validated, fulfilled, shipped and turned into money in every wallet.

Customer
Place the order

On the affiliate store the customer orders Oak Lounge Chair × 1 @ 35,000 DZD, checkout validates wilaya, commune, address & phone. Stock (product or variant) is deducted and low-stock checked.

System
Attach the referral

The order stores referred_by = affiliate code — a single field that tells the system exactly who earns on each leg.

System
Status begins Pending

Admins and the referring affiliate are notified of the new order.

4.2 · Revenue split at delivery

On Delivered, the chain is resolved and the 35,000 DZD is divided:

LevelBuys atSells atMargin / unit
Provider (supplier)—20,000 → Loft+20,000
Loft (Admin)20,00026,000 → affiliate+6,000
Affiliate26,00035,000 retail+9,000
Per-sale settlement

The exact split is computed per unit via compute_item_profit over the resolved chain (supplier → loft → affiliate → semi). On delivery, credit_wallets_for_order writes each share into each wallet, logs a Transaction, and notifies every earner. When a provider’s affiliate sells the provider’s own product, the provider earns the network margin (purchase↔wholesale difference) in place of Loft’s wholesale margin, while Loft keeps its fixed commission.
